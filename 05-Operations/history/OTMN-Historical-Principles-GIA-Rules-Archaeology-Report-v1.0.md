# OTMN Historical Principles & GIA Rules — Archaeology Report v1.0

## Purpose

This document preserves the historical development of OTMN's operating principles and GIA rules during the organization's early formation.

It is a **historical record, not a new operating standard**. The purpose is to show how ideas developed in working discussions were evaluated, consolidated, promoted into GitHub standards, or deliberately left experimental.

## Archaeology Finding

The review of historical OTMN/GIA discussions against the current GitHub documentation found that most important foundational ideas were not lost. They were either:

- promoted into current standards,
- consolidated into broader principles,
- embedded into operational procedures, or
- intentionally left as experimental concepts until further evidence is available.

This is an important distinction: an idea being absent as a standalone rule does not necessarily mean it was forgotten.

## Important Promoted Concepts

### Do not manufacture certainty

A recurring operating behavior became a formal GIA rule: when authoritative information cannot distinguish between alternatives, GIA should not invent a ranking or rationale. The information gap should be reported and human judgment requested where appropriate.

### Capture broadly, promote selectively

The organization deliberately separates the capture of ideas from the decision to make them active work. This principle prevents the idea inventory from becoming an uncontrolled project queue.

### Let the next layer earn its way into existence

Repeated discussion established a broader principle: new standards, taxonomy, automation, dashboards, and other infrastructure should be created only when real work demonstrates that the additional layer is useful.

## Concepts Still Under Validation

The archaeology also identified ideas that should **not** be promoted into current standards yet.

### Observe → Think → Measure → Nurture

A candidate OTMN operating loop currently being tested through real work. Its current status is validation, not formal principle.

### Cookie Jar / Leave an Idea, Take an Idea

An experimental concept for a low-friction commons of unfinished intellectual starting points. Research supports the underlying mechanics, but the concept is now moving into a small practical experiment rather than formalization.

## Historical Interpretation

The most significant finding is that OTMN's operating system has evolved through **promotion and consolidation**, not simple accumulation.

A behavior may begin as:

> observation → discussion → repeated use → evidence → rule/principle

When a narrower rule proves broadly useful, it may be consolidated into a higher-level principle rather than preserved as a separate standard.

This explains why the current GitHub documentation can appear simpler than the historical discussion that produced it: **the simplification is evidence of maturation, not loss.**

## Relationship to the OTMN Core Idea

The archaeology is consistent with the current OTMN Core Idea: OTMN exists to provide a foundation in which good ideas can be developed, tested, connected, and allowed to grow into useful things.

The historical record therefore supports a broader interpretation of the organization:

> **OTMN does not need to know in advance which ideas will matter. It needs a disciplined way to capture them, learn from them, and allow evidence to determine what deserves development.**

## What This Document Does Not Do

This report does not:

- create new OTMN principles;
- modify the GIA Standard;
- create a new audit framework;
- promote experimental ideas into formal policy;
- require historical practices to remain current.

Its purpose is preservation and traceability.
